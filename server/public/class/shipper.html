Shipper.

Craft is your strongest working stat: you edit, commit, open pull requests and publish.

// what makes a Shipper

Craft counts edits, commits, pull requests, published pages and projects touched in the last 30 days. When it beats Appetite, Arsenal and Autonomy, you are a Shipper. Grind and Brains never set the class.

In the Mac menu bar panel, every class has its own colour and hat.

// six stages

Every creature starts as an Egg and grows with the XP you earn. Your class can change as you work; your stage only goes up.

  • Egg from Lv 1
  • Hatchling from Lv 5
  • Lil Guy from Lv 15
  • Big Guy from Lv 30
  • Gigachad from Lv 45
  • GOAT from Lv 60

// grow the rest

To grow the other stats too, run /pet learn: five lessons per stat that tick themselves from your logs.
