  3. Say yes once. It shows exactly what it sends, then joins the board and accepts the duel together.

How the duel works

It starts at 00:00 UTC the day after you accept and runs seven days. Your duel XP counts from your first check-in on or after the day you accept, so XP saved up from before never counts. Check in each day, or turn on auto check-in and it happens by itself.

You two see each other's generated name, level, duel XP, six stats and sus mark. No names, prompts, code or e-mail ever leave your machine. A duel does not put you on the public lists.

The first to accept takes the seat. If someone got there before you, ask your friend for a new link.
