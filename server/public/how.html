01

Install

In Claude Code, paste these two lines:

  • /plugin marketplace add Spider333/cclevel
  • /plugin install cclevel@cclevel

Needs Python 3.9 or newer, nothing else. From a shell instead: claude plugin install cclevel --marketplace Spider333/cclevel

Already installed? Plugins from this marketplace do not update by themselves until you say so: /plugin → Marketplaces → cclevel → Update marketplace, then /reload-plugins. Pick Enable auto-update there too, and new features arrive on their own.

03

Join the board

Say /pet join the leaderboard. It shows you exactly what it would send and asks first. Joining doesn't list you publicly. To be ranked on the world lists, say /pet put me on the world list. You show up after check-ins on two different days.

Check in once a day with /pet check in, or let it happen by itself: /pet check in automatically runs one quiet check-in a day when Claude Code starts (off until you say so). The board ranks only XP it watched you earn after joining, at most 550 a day, so a long history or an edited file doesn't buy a top spot.

04

Bring friends

  1. Start a group: /pet start a group. You get an invite link and a made-up crew name, like Stormy Lemur Crew.
  2. Send the link to friends. Opening it shows the crew's name and how to join. The code after # stays in the browser.
  3. They paste it into Claude Code: /pet join <link>. One yes joins the board and the group.
  4. Compare: /pet show my group. Up to 25 members see each other's names, levels, XP and stats. Nobody else does. Whoever earns the most XP this week wears the ♛ crown; weeks start Monday, 00:00 UTC.

Anyone with the link can join until the group is full, so share it with friends only. If it leaks, start a new group.

Talk to /pet

Just say what you want. Anything that sends something asks first.

SayWhat happens
/petYour card. Offline.
/pet join the leaderboardJoin the board (shows the data, asks first)
/pet check inUpdate your row, once a day
/pet check in automaticallyOne quiet check-in a day when Claude Code starts; stop checking in automatically turns it off
/pet put me on the world listBe ranked publicly; off works at once
/pet rank meYour place, the XP to pass the next one, and how rare your badges are; worked out on your machine, uploads nothing
/pet start a groupA private group and its invite link
/pet join <link>Join a friend's group
/pet show my groupYour crew's leaderboard and this week's ♛
/pet duelChallenge one friend: seven days, most XP wins the ♛. /pet duel <link> accepts one
/pet cardYour card as a picture to save and post; drawn in your browser, sends nothing
/pet wrappedYour year in review from your own ledger: XP, best day, longest streak, level gained, how your stats moved; sends nothing
/pet leave the boardDelete your row at once
06

What gets sent

Everything the board gets from you

{
  "v": 1,
  "xp": 21480,
  "xp_days": 94,
  "stats": { "grind": 55, "appetite": 45, "arsenal": 50,
             "brains": 70, "autonomy": 25, "craft": 55 },
  "badges": ["first_hook", "first_skill", "streak_7"]
}

Plus a random key made on your machine, so your check-ins land on the same row. The board keeps only its hash.

Only if you ask for them: a private group's or a duel's invite code (the board keeps only its hash) and the GitHub login for the badge.

Never sent: your name, e-mail, prompts, code, file paths, or the names of your projects and skills. The board stores no IP address. Your creature's name is made up from a random key on your machine.

Don't trust us, read it: the code and every field the board stores.

How it scores

XP is earned per finished day, at most 550 a day, and sets your level from 1 to 99. Six stats from 0 to 100 add up to your Aura:

StatWhat it measures
GrindDays used out of the last 30, and streaks
AppetiteWeighted tokens over the last 30 days
ArsenalSkills, connectors and subagent types actually used
BrainsTen checks of wiring that is in use
AutonomySessions that ran without you, and scheduled jobs that call Claude
CraftEdits, commits, pull requests and published pages

08

Fine print

How ranking works

Season is the XP a creature earned this month; All-time is everything since it joined. Both count only daily XP the board credited itself, at most 550 per active day, without badge XP. Level is shown as each owner's own scanner reports it, and does not set the order. Equal XP shares a place. The top 1,000 are listed; below them the board publishes the XP needed for each band, and --rank works out your place on your own machine. Lists are rebuilt every hour.

What sus means

A creature with at least 14 witnessed days of which 80% or more were credited at the full 550 XP. It changes nothing else: not the place, not the XP, not the listing. It cannot catch a script that claims a little less, and does not pretend to.

The GitHub badge

Optional. ✓ login · 123 means the owner proved that GitHub account is theirs, and 123 of its public commits carry Claude Code's co-author line. It never changes a place or XP. The count sees public default branches only, counts a squash merge once, and cannot tell a hand-typed line from a real one.

Privacy

The board holds no account, e-mail address or IP address. A creature is its score (nine numbers and a list of badges), a generated name, the hash of a random token and a few dates and counters. It appears on the world lists only while its owner keeps the world ranking on; --world off and --leave take it off at once. Totals count every creature with check-ins on two days, listed or not, and appear only from 100 creatures. PRIVACY.md lists every field.
